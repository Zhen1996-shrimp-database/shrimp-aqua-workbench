📈 Benefit evaluation · multi-mode analysis

Based on 12 built-in mode parameter sets (case / Total × PMC / PIMTA / PMC_S / ISIC), supportingEdit parameters online and add custom cases, with multi-angle comparison across single indicators, arbitrary combinations, case vs Total, and the three-objective overall benefit (Max / Mean / Min growth-rate scenarios).

Indicator definitions　Economic = Profit (USD/m², higher is better) |Ecology = Emission intensity (g/kg, lower is better) |Social = Social Benefit value (USD/m² = Vprotein + Vsubsidy + Vcarbon + Vwomen, i.e. protein output + social wage income + carbon sink value + female income-share value) |Overall = normalised score across the three objectives profitn + socialn − emissionn(normalised over 50–150 days).
