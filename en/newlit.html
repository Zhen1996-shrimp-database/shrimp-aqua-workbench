📄 112 m² Wetland–recirculating aquaculture system (RAS) culture for 90 d at four densities: 90/135/180/360 ind/m³. The vertical-flow wetland was effective at removing ammonium nitrogen, nitrite nitrogen and COD, with removal efficiency rising as density increased; nitrate and phosphorus removal, however, declined as density increased. Large shrimp (9–16 cm; 1.16–3.67 g) were more sensitive to density effects (grey relational grade 0.819), while small shrimp (4–9 cm; 16.37–19.04 g) were less sensitive (0.667). The high density of 540 ind/m³ significantly inhibited the growth of small shrimp.
